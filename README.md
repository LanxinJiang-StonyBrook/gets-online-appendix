# GETS Online Appendix

Interactive online appendix to "Advancing Greenhouse Gas Reporting with Blockchain and Life Cycle Assessment" by Lanxin Jiang, Yu Gu, Wenjun Yu, and Jun Dai (2026).

View it at https://lanxinjiang-stonybrook.github.io/gets-online-appendix/

The page lets readers trace every figure in the paper's life-cycle emission report (Table 3) to its inputs, ledger records, transactions, and raw readings from the simulated year.
